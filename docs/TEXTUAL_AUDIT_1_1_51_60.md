## Readings, speakers and interpretation

- Nārada narrates to Vālmīki. Mārīca speaks the actual warning in 51a. Rāvaṇa disregards it in 51b and goes in 52a. कालचोदितः invokes Time or fate, rendered in English as “driven by fate”, with the literal expression disclosed. No new motive or excuse is supplied.
- The two princes in 52b are Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa. The means are Mārīca's illusion. The verse supplies no golden-deer description, so none is inserted.
- In 53a the summary explicitly uses हत्वा of Jaṭāyus. This is translated faithfully, without importing the longer episode's death sequence or invented last words. In 53b “struck down” renders निहतम्. Maithilī is Sītā. Rāma's lament begins only in 54a.
- The funeral clause of 54b continues into the search in 55. The repeated raw 54/55 material appears once in the corrected sequence. No teaching equating bodily difference with moral character is inferred from Kabandha's description.
- Pronouns change referent in 56: Rāma kills and cremates Kabandha; Kabandha goes to heaven and speaks to Rāma. Bracketed names make this transparent. Heavenly movement is presented as narrative content, without a claim of independent verification. No recovered form, curse story or disappearance is invented.
- Kabandha's instruction spans 56b–57a. In 56b the translation introduces Śabarī without moving the next half-verse's imperative. The actual imperative is quoted in 57a. A bracketed pronoun carries forward its object. Nārada's account resumes in 57b.
- **57a lexical variant:** the immutable import reads **श्रमणीं**, the complete numbered parallel **श्रमणाम्**. Both denote a woman ascetic. The imported reading and matching romanization remain explicit. This does not establish a sectarian affiliation or community identity. IIT corroboration retrieved for 57b–58a does not independently verify this earlier word.
- Śabarī's reception occurs in 58a and Hanumān's meeting in 58b. No fruit-tasting episode, caste label, additional speech or comparison with Daśaratha's hospitality is introduced. Vānara remains the narrative's designation.
- Hanumān's word leads to Sugrīva in 59a. Rāma's account crosses 59b–60a. Sugrīva's listening is 60b; his fire-witnessed pact is **61a**, beyond this batch. Unfinished clauses retain their verse boundaries. Brackets disclose supplied words.
- Connected-verse notes, selected modern context and teachings are bilingual, separate fields and separately labelled reader sections. Reflections are editorial inferences, not additional Sanskrit or universal scriptural commands.
